Off Duty — an original Call of Duty interview series built to explore the people and creative minds behind the franchise. The series brought together developers, artists, musicians, studio leaders, and other voices from across the Call of Duty world for conversations that went beyond the game itself.

Each episode created room to explore the people behind the work, from their creative process and personal influences to their careers, interests, and experiences within the industry. The format was intentionally more relaxed and personality-driven, allowing the conversations to feel less like traditional game interviews and more like a look into the people who help shape the culture around Call of Duty.
