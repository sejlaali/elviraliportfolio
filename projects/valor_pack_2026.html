Built Through Resilience. Forged in Valor.

We worked with U.S. Marine Corps veteran and Medal of Honor recipient Kyle Carpenter to bring his story to Call of Duty through the C.O.D.E. Valor Pack. Inspired by his service and recovery, we helped translate elements of his journey into an in-game collection.

100% of Activision's net proceeds from the pack go to the Call of Duty Endowment.

Forged in Valor.

We produced a three-part series documenting the making of the C.O.D.E. Valor Pack, sharing the story behind its creation and the inspiration behind its design.
